03 / A little context

Hi, I'm Dawid.

I'm a writer, speaker, podcaster, and digital creator interested in how ideas travel, how people connect, and how useful things get made.

Here you'll find books and essays, links to things I find interesting, small apps and digital experiments, and occasional opportunities to meet and exchange ideas. I like keeping things thoughtful, practical, and open to discovery.

This is my home base on the internet. Have a question, a recommendation, or an idea for working together? I'd be glad to hear from you.
